# 📁 RoPwned — Roblox Quality-of-Life Suite
**Created by PwnedNomiiix (`@nomiiixdev`)**

**RoPwned** is a free, open-source browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave, and Opera that lets you organize your Roblox Friends list into collapsible folders and save private local notes.

---

## ✨ Features (v1.0.0)
* **Collapsible Friend Folders:** Group your friends into custom folders (*Squad*, *Traders*, *Devs*, or any name you create).
* **Private Local Notes:** Add notes to any friend card so you never forget where you met or who someone is.
* **One-Click Controls:** Easily move friends into folders, remove them, or delete folders anytime.
* **Toolbar Popup:** Toggle RoPwned on/off instantly or jump straight into the official Discord community.

---

## 🔒 100% Safe & Local
* **Zero External Servers:** All folder names and notes are stored strictly on your own computer via `chrome.storage.local`.
* **No Account Access:** RoPwned **never** accesses your Roblox cookies, passwords, or personal data.

---

## 🚀 How to Install
1. Go to the **[Releases Page](https://github.com)** and download **`ropwned-extension.zip`**.
2. Right-click `ropwned-extension.zip` and click **Extract All** so you have a normal folder.
3. Open your browser and go to **`chrome://extensions`** (or `edge://extensions`).
4. Turn ON **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
5. Click **Load unpacked** in the top-left and select the extracted folder.
6. Pin **RoPwned** to your toolbar and refresh your **Roblox Friends** page!
